# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Make room for newcomers to ask questions and contribute.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publishing
someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the same private channel
described in `SECURITY.md`. The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the community.
